# Blind Spots

Known biases and failure modes, probed adversarially before Collapse. Append on discovery; never delete.

- **Survivorship bias** — backtests on the current index miss delisted names.
- **Look-ahead bias** — using info not available at decision time.
- **Narrative seduction** — compelling story inflates confidence. Probe: would I take this trade if the story were boring?
- **Recency bias** — last regime overweighted as current.
- **Anchoring on entry price** — averaging down without Reframe.
- **Crowding risk** — same thesis held across the street; exit liquidity vanishes.
- **Regime change blindness** — model trained on one regime applied to another.
- **Self-attribution bias** — wins → skill, losses → luck. Calibration log corrects.
- **Confirmation in research** — only reading bull or bear cases.
- **Tail neglect** — Gaussian-shaped intuitions for fat-tailed outcomes.
- **Implicit factor bets** — stock-picking that is really a factor tilt in disguise.
- **Story-stock / over-promise risk** — capex-to-market-cap >2x, MOUs presented as binding contracts, KPI redefinitions across reporting periods, founder/CFO turnover, related-party transactions, going-concern flags, retail-financial-podcast circuit. Probe: what is the population base rate of similar candidates reaching commercial / promised state? Use base rate as prior — strong signals (binding offtake, audited reserves, funded covenants, insider $$$ buying) can overpower it. Hedge via cohort basket / index ETF / anchor-plus-satellite / staged sizing rather than auto-rejecting.
- **Valuation-bubble blindness** — current price already implies bull-case terminal multiple; bull thesis is priced in not pre-recognition. Probe: at current price, what implied 2030 EBITDA / multiple does the bull thesis require? Is that already at or above 60th percentile of comp distribution? If yes, recognition is already priced.
- **Microcap fraud / dilution risk** — pre-revenue / sub-$500M-cap names with capex requirements >market cap, tight free-float (<USD 200M), insider ownership concentration, cross-listed thin liquidity. Probe: total free-float USD, insider/management ownership %, capex/cap ratio, history of share-count growth (5y dilution), auditor changes.
- **Factor-blindness on m.probe.edges** — failing to walk macro/geo/regime/correlation/flows/themes/reflexivity/liquidity. Default-on, must mark N/A with reason if a factor is genuinely irrelevant.
