# Strategies

Two committed archetypes. Everything else about portfolio architecture stays open and reframable per inquiry.

## Type A — Resilient Compounder

**Bet shape.** Vehicles that remain valuable to society across most plausible futures, with low risk of becoming irrelevant, and that are currently under or fairly valued.

**Criteria (illustrative, not gating):**
- Ecosystem moat — controls multiple valuable parts that reinforce each other.
- Multi-year free-cash-flow compounding with sober assumptions.
- Optionality preserved — adjacent expansion paths still available.
- Low obsolescence risk under a wide scenario set (tech shift, regulation, geopolitics, taste).
- Sober-case valuation gap — modest assumptions still imply upside.

**What it is not.** Not a "quality" label. The discriminator is durability *across futures*, not historical financial scoring.

**Outcome window.** Years. Compounders are slow.

## Type B — Asymmetric Speculative

**Bet shape.** Vehicles where future demand is great and likely (by deduction), the masses have not yet realised it, and current valuation prices in something close to zero of that demand.

**Criteria (illustrative, not gating):**
- Explicit demand-deduction chain — first-principles argument why demand will be large, not extrapolation of recent moves.
- Supply-side bottleneck — capacity, scarcity, or specific positioning that makes the candidate the natural beneficiary.
- Narrow window before realisation — the asymmetry only exists pre-recognition.
- Asymmetric payoff — limited downside in most paths, large upside if thesis right.
- Drawdown-bounded — sized so the negative path does not break the book.

**What it is not.** Not "high-volatility narrative chasing". The discriminator is a deductively-likely demand thesis the market has not yet priced.

**Outcome window.** Months to a couple of years; thesis-dependent.

## Open considerations (menu, not structure)

These framings are pulled from situationally by the architect-agent or human, alone or in combination. Nothing is committed in advance:

- **Game-theoretic balance** — money must flow somewhere; where do flows accumulate under each future state?
- **Macro regime** — rate path, growth, inflation, fiscal posture, currency stance.
- **Scenario distribution** — base / bull / bear / tail and any other relevant forks; explicit probabilities.
- **Correlation structure** — what moves together, what diverges, when correlations break.
- **Liquidity and capacity** — can we actually take size; what is exit cost.
- **Mandate and time-horizon mix** — short vs long horizon balance, mandate constraints.
- **Tail-hedge availability** — cheap protection vs implicit hedges.
- **Reflexivity** — where positioning, narrative, and price feed each other.

Stay creative and critical. Add framings as they prove useful via `amendments.md`.

## Vehicle openness

Archetype framing applies across stocks, ETFs, crypto, commodities, FX, fixed income, and any other vehicle that delivers the bet shape. The vehicle is a delivery mechanism; the archetype is the bet shape. Do not narrow the search by vehicle until necessary.
